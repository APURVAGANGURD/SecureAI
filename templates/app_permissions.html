App Permission Security Guide

Permission Categories

Critical

Permissions that can directly harm you or steal sensitive data:

  • SMS/MMS access
  • Call log access
  • Contact list access
  • Camera/microphone

High Risk

Permissions that can compromise privacy or security:

  • Location tracking
  • Storage access
  • Phone state/identity
  • Calendar access

Medium Risk

Permissions that may be necessary but require scrutiny:

  • Internet access
  • Network state
  • Wi-Fi connections
  • Bluetooth

Low Risk

Permissions that are generally safe for most apps:

  • Vibration
  • Wake lock
  • Set wallpaper
  • Install shortcuts

Best Practices for App Permissions

Question Everything

Ask "Why does this app need this permission?" If a flashlight app asks for SMS access, that's suspicious.

Use Official Stores

Download apps only from Google Play Store or Apple App Store. They scan for malware.

Regular Audits

Periodically review permissions for installed apps in your device settings.

Read Reviews

Check user reviews for complaints about suspicious behavior or data collection.

Runtime Permissions

Grant permissions only when needed (like camera only when taking photos).

Uninstall Unused Apps

Remove apps you don't use regularly to minimize permission exposure.

Common Apps & Expected Permissions

Flashlight App

Expected: Camera (for flash), Vibration

Suspicious: Internet, Contacts, SMS, Location

Calculator

Expected: None or Storage (for history)

Suspicious: Internet, Contacts, Phone

Social Media

Expected: Camera, Microphone, Storage, Contacts

Suspicious: SMS, Call Log, Body Sensors

Navigation

Expected: Location, Storage, Internet

Suspicious: SMS, Contacts, Camera

Android vs iOS Permission Systems

Android

  • Granular Control: Can deny individual permissions
  • Runtime Permissions: Ask when needed
  • Background Limits: Restrictions on background access
  • Scoped Storage: Limited file access

iOS

  • Privacy Labels: Clear data collection disclosure
  • App Tracking Transparency: Opt-in for tracking
  • Limited Categories: Fewer permission types
  • Sandboxing: Apps isolated from each other

How to Check/Change Permissions

Android

  1. Go to Settings > Apps & notifications
  2. Select the app
  3. Tap Permissions
  4. Toggle permissions on/off

iOS

  1. Go to Settings
  2. Scroll to the app
  3. Tap to view permissions
  4. Toggle permissions on/off
