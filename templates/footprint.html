Understanding Digital Footprints

Your Digital History

Everything you do online creates a permanent record:

  • Social media posts and likes
  • Search engine queries
  • Online purchases
  • Location check-ins

Privacy Risks

Large digital footprints can lead to:

  • Identity theft
  • Targeted advertising
  • Reputation damage
  • Employment issues

Protection Tools

Tools to help manage your footprint:

  • Privacy-focused browsers
  • VPN services
  • Data removal services
  • Social media audits
