    Common Fake Profile Scams

    Romance Scams

    Fake profiles pretend to seek relationships, then ask for money.

    $304 million lost to romance scams in 2020 (FBI)

    Executive Impersonation

    Pose as company executives to trick employees into wiring money.

    $1.8 billion lost to BEC scams in 2020

    Investment Scams

    Fake "successful traders" offer investment opportunities.

    60% increase in investment scams during pandemic

    Giveaway Scams

    Fake celebrity accounts promise prizes for engagement.

    Most common on Instagram and Twitter

    Profile Verification Techniques

    1

    Reverse Image Search

    Use Google Images or TinEye to check if profile pictures appear elsewhere online.

    Google Images TinEye
    2

    Check Social Media Consistency

    Look for the same person on multiple platforms. Real people usually have consistent presence.

    Tip: Search for their name + location or workplace
    3

    Video Call Verification

    Request a quick video call. Scammers often refuse or make excuses.

    Note: Even video calls can be faked with deepfakes
    4

    Check Account History

    Look at when the account was created and its posting history.

    Tip: Real accounts usually have a mix of content types
    5

    Ask Specific Questions

    Questions about local knowledge or mutual connections that would be hard to fake.

    Example: "What's your favorite restaurant near [mutual location]?"

    Platform-Specific Tips

    Facebook Fake Profile Signs

    • Few friends with no mutual connections
    • Profile created recently
    • No tagged photos with friends
    • Limited personal information
    • Friends list includes many people from different countries

    How to Report:

    Go to profile → Click "..." → Find Support or Report Profile → Follow prompts

    Instagram Fake Profile Signs

    • Few posts but many followers (often bought)
    • Generic or stolen photos
    • Bio with suspicious links
    • Following thousands but few follow back
    • Comments are generic ("Great pic!") or spammy

    How to Report:

    Go to profile → Click "..." → Report → Follow prompts

    LinkedIn Fake Profile Signs

    • Vague or exaggerated job titles
    • Few connections in their industry
    • Generic profile photo (stock image)
    • Endorsements from questionable profiles
    • Quickly asks to connect outside LinkedIn

    How to Report:

    Go to profile → Click "More" → Report/Block → Follow prompts

    Dating App Fake Profile Signs

    • Photos seem too professional
    • Quickly wants to move off the app
    • Vague answers about work/location
    • Asks for money or financial help
    • Claims to be overseas or in military

    How to Report:

    Use in-app reporting features immediately if suspicious

    Protective Measures

    Privacy Settings

    Set profiles to private/friends only. Limit who can see your information.

    Limit Personal Info

    Don't share birth date, address, phone number, or financial details publicly.

    Verify Mutual Connections

    Check with mutual friends before accepting requests from unfamiliar people.

    Regular Audits

    Periodically review your friends/followers and remove suspicious accounts.

    Report Suspicious Accounts

    Report fake profiles to help protect others and get them removed.

    Educate Others

    Share knowledge about fake profiles with friends and family, especially seniors.
